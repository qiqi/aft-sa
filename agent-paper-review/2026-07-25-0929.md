## New major comments

1. **The tab:daetotals cavity-L2 a5 cell was computed with the wrong
   averaging window.** Printed 1.1219/0.02259 = mean of the last 500
   CSV **rows** (5000 pseudo-steps); the canon window (pseudo_step ≥
   last−500, 51 samples — the convention every other cell in the table
   follows, verified cell by cell) gives **CL 1.1223 / CD 0.02254**.
   Consequences: (a) the table disagrees with the regenerated polar
   figure, whose `totals()` plots the canon values; (b) the
   family-agreement sentence's "0.7–1.6 counts (0.35–0.7%)" endpoint
   is a window artifact — canon gives ≈0.7–1.1 counts (0.35–0.5%),
   which is a *stronger* result; (c) the uncommitted
   `canon_results.json` entry and the RESPONSES record ("std
   4e-4/4e-5" — the wide window's std; canon is 1e-5/2e-6) carry the
   same numbers. One-line fix + one sentence retouch.

2. **Appendix D's three new figures are numbered C5–C7.** Appendices
   A, B, C, and E all carry `\renewcommand{\thefigure}{X\arabic{
   figure}}` + `\setcounter{figure}{0}`; Appendix D has neither, so
   fig:daesec10/31/60 continue Appendix C's counter (aux: C5, C6, C7,
   pages 81–83) while the main text and the appendix paragraph both
   direct the reader to "the sectional diagnostic sheets of
   Appendix~D". Add the D renew/setcounter (and decide whether
   tab:daetotals should become Table D1 — it currently prints as
   main-sequence Table 12, a pre-existing choice this change would
   surface).

3. **The stated section Reynolds number is wrong by 30% and
   contradicts the figures it captions.** fig:daesec10's caption says
   "$Re_c=6.6\times10^5$" and the slope paragraph says "the sections'
   $Re\approx6\times10^5$" — but the chord is constant 0.917 m from
   root to the η=0.88 taper break, and Mach/muRef from the case
   Flow360.json gives Re = 5.0×10^5 exactly at all three stations
   (η=0.10/0.31/0.60); the committed sheets' own suptitles print
   "$Re_c=5e+05$". (The cf16922 RESPONSES "Re_c ~ 6.6e5" is the same
   error.) The Sec.~epphandover analogy survives — 5×10^5 is still
   2.5× the Eppler benchmark — but the number must match the figure.

4. **The 9–10-count "does not tile" accounting note is not
   reproducible from committed code, and its attribution appears
   wrong.** The text: "the strip decomposition does not tile the
   wetted surface---its integral sits a nearly incidence-independent
   9–10 counts above the force totals." Chord-weighted integration of
   the RANS sectional cd (the committed figure's own strips) lands
   **1 count below** the force totals (−1.0/−1.2/−1.0 at 4/5/6°) — the
   RANS strips tile essentially perfectly; it is the *reference's*
   sectional curve that under-integrates its own ledger total (−18 to
   −21 counts under the figure's cd_ref recipe), so the Δ(sectional)
   vs Δ(total) offset I measure is +17–20 counts, not 9–10. The 9–10
   came from an uncommitted computation (RESPONSES: Δstrip
   −3.7/+1.8/+8.3 vs Δforce −12.8/−7.2/−2.1) whose integration weights
   I could not reconstruct. The *qualitative* moral ("sectional slopes
   are trustworthy where sectional levels are not") stands under
   either accounting, but the quantified sentence needs (a) the
   diagnosis integral committed as a repro script — house rule for
   every other number in the paper — and (b) the "sits above the
   force totals" attribution corrected to whichever accounting that
   script actually implements.

5. **The appendix paragraph describes sheet content the sheets do not
   draw.** "the probe-maximum $Re_\Omega$ against its onset
   threshold" — no threshold curve appears in row 1 of any sheet, and
   the generator imports the onset constants (`C_ON, A_ON, B_ON,
   K_ON`) without ever using them: a planned overlay that was dropped.
   Either add the overlay (the constants are sitting there) or drop
   "against its onset threshold". In the same sentence, "(log scale,
   as Fig.~2)" is a hardcoded literal figure number — it currently
   resolves correctly to the onset-graze figure, but it is the only
   internal literal in the paper (pass-21 verified all others are
   external-document references) and will silently rot; use
   `\ref{fig:onsetgraze}`.

6. **The section-sheet captions and appendix text assert dashed
   unstructured-L2 curves that exist only in the α=4° column.**
   fig:daesec10's caption ("structured L2 solid, unstructured L2
   dashed... at α=4°, 5°, 6°") and the appendix paragraph ("dashed
   the unstructured-family L2") carry no availability qualifier; the
   committed sheets show dashed curves at 4° only (a5's field output
   is a disk-full casualty, a6 is running) — only the in-figure legend
   says "where complete". Relatedly, fig:daepolar's pass-21-fixed
   "finest completed grid per family and incidence" is now itself
   unseated at α=5°: cav-L2 a5 is *complete* by the paper's own ledger
   ("the incidences where both finest grids are complete") yet the
   sectional panel falls back to cav-L1 via `has_strips()`. One
   disclosure clause fixes both, and both go stale again (in the good
   direction) when `recover_a5_outputs.sh` runs — worth wording so the
   recovery doesn't need a third touch.
