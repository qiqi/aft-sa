## New major comments

1. **The polar caption's family-agreement band is stale for what now
   plots.** "the two mesh families agree to within ΔC_d ≤ 2×10⁻⁴
   (0.1–0.2×10⁻³ across the four incidences)" (l. 1836–1838): with ten
   incidences on the L2 pair, the recomputed band is 0.2–2.4×10⁻⁴ over
   −2…7° — the α=5° gap (2.4×10⁻⁴) already sits marginally above the
   stated ceiling — and **1.0×10⁻³ at 8.5°**, five times the stated bound.
   Re-scope: e.g. "≤2.4×10⁻⁴ across −2°–7°; 1.0×10⁻³ at the unsteady
   8.5° point", or keep the tight band explicitly for the bucket and call
   out 8.5° separately.
2. **"Tracks the measured low-drag bucket across the range at matched
   lift: 3.7–6.4% below…" (l. 1852–1858) no longer describes the plotted
   range.** At the new −2° point the computed drag sits **~14% below** the
   measured curve at matched lift (computed c_l 0.175/0.179, c_d
   0.0109/0.0108 vs experimental ≈0.0126–0.0127 interpolated between the
   α=−2.84 and −1.99 rows): the model's bucket extends below c_l≈0.25
   where the LTPT's low-drag corner has already ended. At 8.5° matched
   lift is ill-posed (cav CL 1.243 exceeds the maximum measured CL 1.231;
   the computed point is a median over an unsteady window near the knee).
   Either scope the sentence to α = 0°–7° (c_l 0.38–1.15), where the
   3.7–6.4% band is verified, or add the two endpoint caveats. The
   RESPONSES line "tracks the measured bucket from cl 0.18 to the 8.5-deg
   knee" overstates on the same grounds.
3. **The twelve L2 extension solutions are plotted (Figs. 11 and 14) but
   never introduced or discussed in the body.** The Eppler setup paragraph
   (l. 1803–1809) still reads "across α∈{0°,2°,5°,7°} … twenty-four
   cases"; contrast the NLF section, which has the exact parallel sentence
   for its negative pair ("The finest grids extend the matrix to…",
   l. 1470–1475). Worse, the most interesting new result — at 8.5° both
   families reproduce the post-stall LE-bubble *topology* (str
   0.032–0.038c, cav 0.020–0.049c) but reattach ~4× short of the oil
   flow's 0.03–0.18c, extending the α≥7 early-reattachment trend past the
   stall — appears only as an unexplained pair of markers on Fig. 14 whose
   caption explains the *experimental* 8.5° point but not the computed
   ones. One setup sentence plus 2–3 sentences after the 7° discussion
   (l. 2033) would close both gaps. The summary paragraph's "another
   twenty-four cases" (l. ~2727) also undercounts what the figures now
   carry.
