TM-4062 was NOT in the repo (references/ had every other E387 paper but not
McGhee). Fetched from NASA NTRS. It is a 234-page scan with an OCR text layer;
the numeric OCR is unreliable, so every value below was read **visually** from

the pages).

### Selection rule (recorded in the JSON)
For the alpha=5 sweep, every **increasing-alpha** Table B1 row with
`|alpha − 5.00| ≤ 0.10 deg` is one individual measurement = one black circle.
Hysteresis (decreasing-alpha) runs contribute nothing (their nearest rows are
~4.0 and ~6.0 deg). This is a clean, principled criterion — no cherry-picking.
