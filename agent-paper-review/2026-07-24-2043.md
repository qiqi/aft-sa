## New comments

1. **Sentence fragment at the Appendix D edit (l. 3210–3219).** The old
   sentence was "induced drag is taken from the Trefftz-plane total,
   distributed as $c_l\alpha_i$ (…), and profile drag is XFOIL…". The
   rewrite closed the new parenthetical as its own sentence
   ("…$24\times80$.)") but kept the continuation, so the source now
   reads "…moving $0.2\%$ from $12\times40$ to $24\times80$.)\nand
   profile drag is\nXFOIL at each station's…" — "and profile drag is
   XFOIL…" is a fragment opening on a lowercase "and" after a closed
   period-parenthesis. Fix either way: demote the parenthetical back
   inside the sentence (drop the period before "(", end "…$24\times80$),
   and profile drag is…"), or promote the continuation ("Profile drag
   is XFOIL at…").

2. **"$C_{D\mathrm{ind}}\!=\!C_{D\mathrm{ff}}$ to four digits at every
   lattice" (l. 3214–3216) holds as decimal places, not significant
   figures, on the coarser reading.** From my DP rerun: 12×40 agrees to
   0.009% (genuinely four significant digits), but 16×56 is
   0.0092541 vs 0.0092582 (0.044%) and 24×80 is 0.0092564 vs 0.0092667
   (0.11%) — the fourth significant figure differs at both. As four
   *decimal* places (i.e. within ~0.1 drag count) the claim is true
   everywhere. A referee who rebuilds AVL in DP will see 9256 vs 9267.
   "to within a tenth of a count at every lattice" or "to three to four
   digits" is unassailable.

3. **The 0.2% lattice movement is quoted from the SP cache but now sits
   in a DP sentence (l. 3216–3218).** "$C_{D\mathrm{ff}}$ … moving
   $0.2\%$ from $12\times40$ to $24\times80$" is 0.177% on the
   committed SP pair (0.0092442 → 0.0092606) but 0.245% on the DP
   ladder (0.0092440 → 0.0092667) that the rest of the sentence is
   about. Both round defensibly to "0.2%" only if one rounds half-down;
   "≲0.25%" or "a quarter percent" covers both builds, or keep 0.2% and
   let the SP cache be the cited pair.

4. **The double-precision evidence is entirely untracked.** a6d5a49
   commits no artifact of the DP run: `tools/avl/` (source tarball,
   Makefile with the flags, build.log, binary) is untracked, as are the
   working leftovers `daedalus/avl/dae_conv.avl` (the abandoned 32×120
   input) and `daedalus/avl/.txt`. The only committed record of the
   ladder is RESPONSES.md prose. The paper now makes three quantitative
   claims from that run (31%, NaN, four digits). I could verify only
   because the binary happens to survive on this disk. Track the three
   DP FT outputs (e.g. `daedalus/avl/ft_dp{1240,1656,2480}.txt`) plus
   the two ladder `.avl` inputs and a one-line note of the build flags

   `build_avl_dp.sh`. Delete or name the orphan `daedalus/avl/.txt`
