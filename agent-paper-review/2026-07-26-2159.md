histories under the section's stated window (final-500-pseudo-step mean, 51

untouched — and the AVL reference was not actually retrimmed to the fv1
lifts (caches dated Jul 24, `canon_cl()` still reads the old tree).
Compile is clean: 113 pp, 0 undefined refs, 0 overfulls (with the broken
sheets embedded). Conclusion and abstract survive untouched — their
Daedalus claims (separation ≤ 0.02c, reattachment < 0.01c) still hold at
fv1, barely (0.017c / 0.009c).
