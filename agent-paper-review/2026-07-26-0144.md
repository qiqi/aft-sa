   (Pre-existing string, but the rename pass is precisely when it should have
   been converted along with the row-2 label one screen below it.)

3. **Sign slip in the definitional sentence of "accumulated inflection"
   (line 481–484).** "the integrand $\tfrac12 s^2 u'''$ is the
   third-derivative contribution to the wall-to-point change of the
   vorticity, and $I$ accumulates it". With this section's convention
   ω=−u′ (stated a few lines below), the third-derivative (ω″) contribution
   to ω(d)−ω(0) is +½s²ω″ = **−**½s²u‴ — which is also the actual integrand
   of the stated integral I=−∫½s²u‴ds. As written, both the "integrand" and
   the vorticity-change identification are off by a sign; I accumulates
   −½s²u‴, not ½s²u‴. RESPONSES 00:20 has it right (ι(y)=−½y²u‴). Fix:
   "the integrand −½s²u‴ = ½s²ω″ is the third-derivative contribution…".
   Since this sentence is the theorem the new name rests on, flagging it
   major.
