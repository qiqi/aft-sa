## Summary

The new analysis is the strongest kind of limitations section a model
paper can carry — a measured, mechanism-level account of *why* the
model's low-Re successes are compensation — and its core evidence is
solid: every number the committed script prints reproduces exactly
(front surplus 16.03×/28.82× at 60k/100k, 0.03×/0.01× at 300k/460k;
rear 19.50×/2.84× at 300k/460k), my independent 2×10⁵ measurement
(front 0.09×, rear 58×, gated rate 0) lands the flip square on the
bursting boundary as claimed, the reservoir maxima (4.9/17.9 at
60k/100k) support the "crosses χ∼1" clause, and the App. C sheets do
show the bottom-led vs top-led front the text asks the reader to see.
The honesty framing is genuinely plain ("the sum of two canceling
errors, not resolved transition physics"), the Conclusion is updated,
and the two mid-review commits preempted objections I was drafting —
ada238c's removal of the destruction-floor control resolves what would
have been a direct contradiction with l. 899's "tied destruction floor
is inert", and its demotion of the self-diffusion switch to a
parenthetical is the right epistemic call. Four things do not survive
scrutiny. The cancellation paragraph's mechanism clause — "the
re-seeded, too-fast climb places the χ=1 front roughly where the
physical envelope would" — is falsified by the committed data (the
model's front sits 0.16c *ahead* of the envelope's equivalent station,
which is also what the cancellation logic *needs*); the dead-air
production bound "below 10⁻⁹ a_max at every swept Re" is exceeded ~40×
by the script's own 100k output; "the model can still predict the
bubble" collides with the same section's "never closes"; and 1721ddb
re-badges the Daedalus wing's 0.09c front offset — previously and
correctly explained as the benign χ=1-vs-N_crit convention gap — as
"the compensation of Sec. epphandover", at a Reynolds number where the
paper's own measurement says the re-seeding half of that compensation
is off (0.01×). Plotting round: verified regenerated-and-consistent on
all four committed sheets, with one stale sentence left in the body
text ("above the dotted reference" — the dotted reference is gone) and
28 tracked-but-uncommitted side-effect figure PDFs left dirty in the
working tree.

## New major comments

1. **"…the re-seeded, too-fast climb places the χ=1 front roughly
   where the physical envelope would" (cancellation paragraph,
   l. ~2505–2507) is falsified by the committed data — and the
   cancellation needs the opposite.** Measured on the same slices the
   section quotes: the model's near-wall χ=1 front sits at x = 0.408
   (100k) / 0.438 (60k) (consistent with pass-12's 0.41–0.44 from
   `analyze_handover_length.py`), while the physical envelope's
   χ=1-equivalent station — N = ln(1/χ∞) = 7.04 on the committed
   mfoil sweep pickle — is 0.569 (100k) / 0.603 (60k), with mfoil
   transition (N=9) at 0.634/0.676. The re-seeded front is therefore
   **0.16c ahead of** where the physical envelope would put it, about
   a third of the pre-burst bubble — not "roughly where". Note the
   clause also undermines the paragraph's own logic: if the front were
   placed *right* and the handover is over-long, the two errors would
   *add* on the reattachment side; the cancellation exists precisely
   because the front is early. Rewrite to what the data show, e.g.
   "the re-seeded climb fires the χ=1 front ~0.15–0.2c ahead of the
   physical envelope, a head start the over-long handover then spends
   (and, below the boundary, overspends)."

2. **The dead-air production bound is exceeded ~40× by the script's
   own output at 10⁵.** Text (l. ~2456–2458): "median effective rate
   below 10⁻⁹ a_max at every swept Re"; script docstring: "median
   effective rate ≤ 1e-10". Measured (this rerun, on data that
   predates the commits): median rate = 1.07×10⁻¹⁰ (60k) ✓, 0 (300k,
   460k) ✓, but **7.34×10⁻⁹ at 100k** — and since the script's `rate`
   already includes the a_max factor, that is 3.9×10⁻⁸ a_max, ~40×
   over the tex bound under the natural reading (and 70× over the
   docstring's). The conclusion survives comfortably — even the 95th
   percentile (7×10⁻⁵) is four orders below the free-shear rate, so
   "advection–diffusion fed, not amplification fed" stands — but the
   printed bound must match the printout. "below 10⁻⁷ a_max"
   (covers 3.9×10⁻⁸) or "≤ 10⁻⁸" in absolute rate units both hold at
   every Re; fix the docstring's stale "≤ 1e-10" in the same pass.

3. **"At Re ≲ 10⁵ the model can still predict the bubble and the gross
   aerodynamics" (l. ~2503–2505) contradicts the section it sits in.**
   Two pages earlier the same section states the 10⁵ state is "the
   non-closing one" and `tab:epphandover` prints "no closure" at 10⁵
   and 6×10⁴; `tab:eppfork` shows c_d = 0.0341 vs measured 0.0237
   (+44%) on the unstructured family and c_l 12% low on the
   structured. What the low-Re solutions get right is the transition
   *front*, the separation point, and (family-dependently) the lift —
   not "the bubble", which never closes. Scope the sentence to what
   the compensation actually delivers, e.g. "can still place the
   bubble's front and land within ~10% on lift", or the honest-caution
   paragraph undercuts itself at its one load-bearing sentence.

4. **1721ddb re-attributes the Daedalus wing's 0.09c front offset from
   a benign convention gap to "the compensation of
   Sec.~\ref{sec:epphandover} seen in three dimensions" — at a chord
   Reynolds number where the paper's own measurement says the
   re-seeding half of that compensation is off.** The replaced text
   explained the offset quantitatively and innocently: χ=1 sits
   ln(1/χ∞)=11.7 e-folds up the envelope vs the strips'
   N_crit = 13.6, and the in-bubble envelope climbs ~2 e-folds per
   0.09c — the offset *is* the seed-convention arithmetic. At the
   wing's Re ≈ 5×10⁵ the front surplus is 0.01× (the 4.6×10⁵ row):
   no re-seeding, so the "books its e-folds early" half there is
   convention, not the low-Re defect pair Sec. V.B describes. As
   rewritten, a careful reader will conclude the wing exhibits the
   two-defect compensation — the section's own data say it doesn't.
   Keep the mechanism sentence if desired, but restore the one-line
   convention arithmetic as the *reason* the front is early at this
   Re (and reserve "the compensation of Sec. V.B" for the
   handover-length half, which is real at every Re).
