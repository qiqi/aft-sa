## New major comments

1. **CRITICAL — the paper now defines a model its results sections
   don't use, and nowhere says so.** Sec. II.F presents the bypass
   as canon (unhedged present tense), Appendix E's μt assembly
   carries it, and `canonical_ai_env()` exports it — but every
   printed figure, table, and quoted number in the paper is from
   `flow360_fr`, computed bypass-OFF (the `flow360_fv1` root is
   24/93 done). Worse, II.F closes with "its consequences are
   quantified with the Reynolds sweep of Sec.~eppresweep" — that
   section contains no bypass-ON data and no ON/OFF comparison; a
   reader will take tab:eppresweep's 1e5 row (0.769/0.0444,
   0.860/0.0341) as the with-bypass model, when the battery record
   says the bypass moves exactly that condition by tens of counts
   (linear form: 0.925/0.0253 on the structured L2). Until the root
   flip and full refresh land, either (a) hold II.F/Appendix E out
   of the tex, or (b) add an explicit status sentence ("the results
   of Secs. IV–VII were computed with the bypass inactive; see …")
   — the current state is the one configuration that can mislead.
   The RESPONSES ~17:20 plan itself says the migration happens "as
   its own reviewed pass"; this commit front-ran the definition half.

2. **The eleven content-changed figures were regenerated but never
   committed — HEAD is self-inconsistent.** 6b463fd changes the four
   regen scripts and the fig:eppcfhigh caption ("XFOIL's dumped Cf
   … is converted to the freestream convention"), but commits no
   PDFs; the corrected figures sit in the working tree (mtime
   14:19, two minutes before the commit), and HEAD's committed
   `eppler_cf_highalpha*.pdf`, `nlf_cf_highalpha*.pdf`,
   `eppler_resweep_{low,high}Re.pdf`, `daedalus_section_eta*.pdf`
   still show the unconverted (up to 2.2× inflated) dotted curves —
   directly contradicting the new caption. Anyone building the paper
   from a fresh clone gets caption-vs-figure disagreement. The
   working-tree copies verify against the committed scripts
   (content-stream identical on regeneration) — commit them (the 11
   date-only siblings can go along or be checked out, either way).

3. **Two of Sec. II.F's inertness/margin claims overreach, and the
   promised analytic note was not delivered.** (a) "in attached
   layers $G$ never opens" is false at the outer edge of any
   attached turbulent layer: where χ descends through the (1,30)
   band near y≈δ, the reconstruction y⁺=χ/κ is small while
   Re_u=U·y/ν is O(δ⁺U⁺_e), so q ≫ 4 and w→1, inflating μt by
   1/fv1 (up to ~4× at χ=5) in a band where the identity is void in
   the *other* direction. The battery bounds the consequence
   empirically (flat plate/NLF ≤1.5 counts, mostly ≤0.4), so the
   fix is wording, not model: claim inertness of the *inner*
   attached layer and cite the flat-plate/NLF invariance for the
   edge band. (b) "which a composite log–wake profile bounds below
   two in the gated band": checked numerically (Reichardt +
   (2Π/κ)sin²(πη/2), band χ∈(1,30) ⇒ y⁺≤73), the bound holds for
   Π≤3.5 at δ⁺≥100 (max q 1.9) but fails for near-separation wake
   parameters — Π≈7 at δ⁺≈100 gives q≈2.8, i.e. G≈0.4 in an
   attached layer. And the RESPONSES ~18:15 entry promised "the
   analytic q-margin composite-profile note goes to repro/analytic"
   — no such file exists (grep: no Reichardt/composite-q anywhere
   under `paper/repro/analytic/`). Deliver the note with the actual
   Π/δ⁺ envelope and scope the sentence to it.

4. **fig:nlfcfhigh caption misattributes the α=9/15 reference — the
   same error class this commit just fixed in fig:eppcfhigh.** The
   caption says "both the $e^9$ overlay (dash-dot: $C_p$, $C_f$, and
   transition location) and the … $N(x)$ … are FlexFoil's". In
   `regen_nlf_v2.py` the N envelope is FlexFoil's (`ffn`, the
   `_ff_fill` branch) but the dash-dot Cp/Cf overlay and the xtr
   lines are the XFOIL pickle (`xf`, `_xfoil_fill`) — exactly why
   the RESPONSES fix list says "NLF five-row sheets where the XFOIL
   fill is used (alpha=9/15)". Correct the attribution, and note the
   ×ue² conversion here too (these are among the curves that just
   changed; see minor 4).
