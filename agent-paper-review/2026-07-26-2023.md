## New major comments

1. **α=29.7° waterfall offsets are Fig. 4's, not Fig. 5's.** Stock displaces
   the α=29.7° curves by ΔCft = −3.0 and Δγ = −20° (his text, confirmed from
   Fig. 5's axes); the script's module constants `DCF, DGAM = -1.5, -15.0`
   are applied to both incidences, and the H4 caption claims "presentation of
   Stock's Fig.~5". Consequence is visible in the committed figure: the
   α=29.7° curves tangle heavily and the right-edge station labels collide
   (X/a = 0.304 / 0.65 overprint). Stock widened the offsets at 29.7°
   precisely because the physical range is larger. Per-incidence offsets are
   needed before the task-37 measured symbols (digitized from Fig. 5's
   geometry) can be overlaid consistently.
   (`paper/repro/cfd/regen_spheroid_station_profiles.py` line 25;
   `paper/sa-ai.tex` H4 caption and the appendix intro's "−1.5×10⁻³ and
   −15° per station" sentence, which also states one offset pair for both.)
2. **α=29.7° hot-film station 5 is X/a = 0.130 in Stock's Fig. 5, not
   0.304.** Fig. 4 (α=10°) lists 0.304; Fig. 5's legend (verified at 600 dpi)
   lists 0.130 in that slot — the DFVLR hot-film array sat at a different
   azimuthal/axial position for the 29.7° run. The script uses the single
   `HF_STATIONS` list for both incidences, so the H4 curves at "0.304" have
   no measured counterpart in Fig. 5 and task 37 would digitize symbols at
   0.130 with no computed curve to land on.
3. **The Cp displacement 0.14 reproduces Stock's text but not his figures —
   and the figures are the presentation the appendix claims.** Stock's prose
   says "displaced by a factor ΔCp = 0.14", but pixel digitization of both
   Figs. 2 and 3 gives a uniform per-station displacement of ≈ 0.42
   (= 3×0.14 — almost certainly an error in Stock's text). With +0.14 the
   committed H1/H3 do not look like the DFVLR presentation: the physical
   aft-station gaps are only ~0.02, so curves X/a = −0.288…0.792 crowd at
   ~0.15 spacing (clearly visible in H1) where Stock's are evenly ~0.44
   apart. The RESPONSES claim "exact conventions of Stock Figs 2-5 (station
   lists from the figure legends)" fails on this plus majors 1–2. Recommend
   Δ(−Cp) = 0.42 (state it as measured from the figures, with a footnote on
   Stock's 0.14), since the task-37 symbol digitization will inherit whatever
   offset the figure grid actually uses.

## New minor comments

1. **Station 1 (X/a = −0.9988) is silently extrapolated out of the probe
   domain.** x/L = 0.0006 lies below the surface-map grid start
   `XLO = 0.004`; `station_curve`'s clipped `searchsorted` yields f = −1.23,
   a linear extrapolation ~1.2 cells into the steepest gradient on the body.
   The extrapolated stagnation value Cp = 1.012 exceeds the M=0.1 bound
   1.0025 by ~0.01. This is the one curve Stock presents as "the correct
   numerical values". Either extend the probe grid below 0.0006 or probe the
   13 stations directly.
2. **Roadmap paragraph not updated**: `sa-ai.tex` lines 3045–3053 still
   describe Appendices A–G only; H is absent.
3. **Front-aft sentence over-generalizes across incidences**: "at
   Re_L = 1.5×10⁶ the front sits aft of the hot-film stations over most of
   the azimuth range" is exact at α=10° (zero squares in H2) but false as a
   blanket statement at α=29.7°, where H4 shows the front crossing every
   hot-film station at φ ≈ 75–145° (stations are turbulent over 20–60% of
   the azimuth). Scope it to α=10° (or to the windward half at 29.7°).
4. **Stock's own two Cp legends disagree at the 4th decimal** (Fig. 2:
   −0.03780/0.21220/0.46180; Fig. 3: −0.03760/0.21240/0.46160 — same taps,
   2×10⁻⁴ X/a apart; the script matches Fig. 3). Physically negligible, but
   worth a one-line note in the task-37 digitization record so the
   discrepancy isn't rediscovered as a digitization error.
5. **Right-edge station annotations collide at α=29.7°** (the φ=181°
   anchored labels for X/a = 0.304 and 0.65 overprint in H4's top panel).
   Fixing major 1's offsets will mostly cure this; otherwise stagger the
   labels or use Stock's legend-box convention.
6. **`front_phi` docstring doesn't match the (correct) implementation**: it
   promises "the smallest phi at which the front x(phi) <= x_station" but the
   code returns all sign-change crossings of fx(φ) − x_s (right for wavy
   fronts). Also `hits[0] > 0` silently drops fronts that reach the first
   x-index, and crossings adjacent to NaN spans are skipped — fine today,
   worth a comment.
7. **Figure ordering and titles**: Stock groups by quantity (Figs. 2–3 Cp,
   then 4–5 Cft/γ); the appendix groups by incidence (H1–H2 α=10°, H3–H4
   α=29.7°) — acceptable, but a reader cross-walking to Stock will notice.
   The script also hardcodes "Re_L = 1.5×10⁶" in titles rather than reading
   the npz `mach/muref` — will mislabel if ever pointed at the re65/re72
   maps.
