The reruns are real, canon-faithful, and complete — and on the
question this pass existed to answer, the paper is honest:
**monostability at 10⁵ survives the bypass**. The interim 08:05
RESPONSES reading (path spreads ~0.10 in $c_l$ — a would-be bistable
band) was correctly held out of the paper until the 40k extensions
decided, and they decided the same way as pass 12→13: every warm path
converges onto the cold state (str spread 0.0030 in CL across all four

prints, residual disclosed inline). The pre-bypass discipline lesson
was applied, not re-learned. The 6×10⁴ forks collapse onto the burst
branch, and the quietly dropped "within 20,000 iterations" clause is
an *improvement* (the str fork is 0.017 from cold and still relaxing —
the pass-13 minor-5 situation again, this time worded honestly). All
pass-33 scope caveats in scope (probes, relaxation time, tautest,
App-C) are replaced with new-canon results; none survive, none were
removed without replacement data; Sec VII's bypass-inactive disclosure
correctly remains.

The one real defect is the τ=1 rewrite: **the printed amplitudes
0.34/0.29 come from a window (final 50%) that is contaminated by the
cold-start transient, and at 2×10⁵ the number is ~5× the recurrent
oscillation**. Under the paper's own prior convention (final-20% ptp,
the one pass 13 verified as 0.287→"≈0.3" and 0.081→"≈0.08"), the
new-canon values are **0.30 and 0.059** — unchanged at 10⁵ and
*weaker* at 2×10⁵. "Where the oscillation is stronger still" is wrong
in direction, and the commit message's "sharpening the memoryless-gate
conclusion" is a convention artifact. The relaxation-oscillator
finding itself (and pass 25's trade-bias-for-oscillation claim)
survives at the new canon — recurrent bursts re-fire at both Reynolds
numbers after near-flat convergence — so only the numbers and the
comparative clause need fixing (major 1).

## New major comments

1. **The τ=1 amplitudes are transient-contaminated and the
   "stronger still" comparison is backwards at 2×10⁵.** The tautest
   runs are cold starts (by design; CL swings −3.5→+4.2 in the first
   10%). The paper's established amplitude convention — final-20% ptp,
   the one pass 13 verified against the pre-bypass runs
   (0.287→"≈0.3", 0.081→"≈0.08") — gives, on the identical-protocol
   new-canon runs: **0.304 at 10⁵ and 0.059 at 2×10⁵**. The printed
   "ΔC_l ≈ 0.34 … and ≈ 0.29" (l. 2324–2325) are the final-*50%* ptp
   (0.337/0.291) — a window whose lower endpoint sits on the monotone
   decay of the startup transient; at 2×10⁵ the history decays to
   *flat* (segment-8 ptp 0.003) before the recurrent burst of ~0.06,
   so the printed 0.29 is ~5× the actual limit-cycle amplitude and is
   mostly the impulsive start. Consequences: (a) "even at the
   otherwise steady 2×10⁵ benchmark" now overstates the oscillation
   there by 5×; (b) "repeated at the recomputed canon, where the
   oscillation is stronger still" (l. 2326–2327) is false under any
   consistent convention — same-convention comparison gives 0.29→0.30
   at 10⁵ (unchanged) and 0.08→0.06 at 2×10⁵ (*weaker*); (c) the
   commit message's "sharpening the memoryless-gate conclusion" is an
   artifact of comparing new final-50% against old final-20%. This is
   the pass-12-major-1 error class (photographing a transient), caught
   at the fork study and reintroduced at the tau study in the same
   commit. Fix: print "≈0.3 at 10⁵ and ≈0.06 at 2×10⁵" and replace
   "where the oscillation is stronger still" with "where the
   oscillation persists" (or "essentially unchanged"); the qualitative
   sentence (relaxation oscillator, bubble reopens) needs no change —
   the recurrence is verified at both Re. While there: align
   `run_tau_test.py`'s docstring RESULT line (still "~0.3 / ~0.08",
   pre-bypass values) with whatever the tex settles on.
