## Short summary

The restructure itself is sound: macroscopic-first ordering works, the honest-framing
sentences from passes 40–41 all survive, the totals table's eleven completed cells
reproduce exactly from the case force CSVs under the paper's median-final-20%
convention, and the newly digitized Stock computed-separation polyline verifies
against Fig. 15a of `paper/spheroid.pdf` to within its stated ±0.01 x/L. The section's
scoping prose, however, has moved ahead of the data in three places: "every condition
with DFVLR hot-film front measurements is computed on the full ladder" is an overclaim
both against the run status (3 of the 7 listed conditions have zero completed solves)
and against the DFVLR record itself (α = 15°, 20°, 24°, 29.5° hot-film conditions in
Stock Figs. 15b/c–16b are measured but not in the matrix at all); "zero lift to within
the force oscillation" at α = 0° is contradicted by the force history (tail CL median
0.0062 with the entire tail positive, min 0.0030); and "a few percent per level"
softens what the pre-restructure text honestly called "not yet grid-converged" (the
α = 10° CL moves −10.6% from L1 to L2). The α = 29.7° ladder landed (all three levels
solved, L2 maps in the section), presented qualitatively only — no front claims are
made, but the deferral sentence forgets that measured fronts exist for it (Stock
Fig. 16c, Re = 1.53×10⁶) and for α = 5° (Fig. 14c, Re = 1.52×10⁶), both fully
computed. Compile is clean (108 pp, no undefined refs, no overfulls).

## New major comments

1. **"Every condition with DFVLR hot-film front measurements is computed on the full
   ladder" (sa-ai.tex:2759–2764) overclaims twice, and the table caption compounds
   it.** (a) Run status: of the seven listed conditions, three (6.5×10⁶ × {5°,10°},
   7.2×10⁶ × 2.5°) have no completed solve at any level and 7.2×10⁶ × 0° has L0 only
   — "is computed" is false at press time; the emitter's `any_done` guard then drops
   those three conditions from the table *silently*, so the caption's "at every
   DFVLR-measured condition ... Dashes: runs in progress" describes rows the reader
   cannot see (a condition with zero completed levels shows neither row nor dashes).
   (b) Scope: the DFVLR hot-film record as reported by Stock (Ref. 49 throughout
   Figs. 14–16) also includes α = 10° at 6.56×10⁶, 15° at 6.54×10⁶, 20° at 6.53×10⁶,
   24° at 6.42×10⁶, 29.5° at 3.01/4.48/8.52×10⁶, and 29.7° at 6.54×10⁶ — none in the
   matrix. Say what is true: "the pure-TS anchors, the low-Re ladder, and the
   workshop pair — the measured conditions spanning Stock's three regimes" (or
   similar), make the emitter print dash rows for staged conditions instead of
   dropping them (and assert on unknown keys, as the NLF/Eppler emitters already do),
   and drop "every".
2. **The α=0° "zero lift to within the force oscillation" claim (2769–2771) is
   contradicted by its own force history.** L0 re72a0 tail (final 20%): CL median
   +0.0062, oscillation σ 0.0017, min +0.0030 — the entire tail is positive, ~4σ from
   zero, and CL/CD ≈ 0.4. The history also trends upward (window medians +0.0016 →
   +0.0077 over the last half), and the RESPONSES.md entry for this very commit flags
   "check the tail for a slow transient at alpha=0" — the paper sentence asserts the
   opposite of the open concern. Either let the run converge (or diagnose the
   symmetry-breaking) before claiming zero, or state the number: "carries residual
   lift C_L ≈ 0.006, still decaying/under investigation."
3. **"The attached-flow incidences move by a few percent per level" (2767–2768)
   softens the pre-restructure disclosure.** The deleted text said the totals are
   "not yet grid-converged"; the actual moves are: a5 CL −7.6% (L0→L1), −6.6%
   (L1→L2); a10 CL +1.3% then **−10.6%**; a5 CD +6.6% (L1→L2); a10 CD ±4%. A
   reviewer with the table in front of them (it is directly below the sentence) will
   compute 10.6% and read "a few percent" as spin. Say "several percent per level,
   up to 11% in C_L at α=10° on the finest step — not yet grid-converged" or
   equivalent.
4. **The deferral sentence (2808–2810) forgets the two completed 1.5×10⁶ conditions
   with measured fronts.** It promises per-condition comparisons only for "the 6.5
   and 7.2×10⁶ solutions of Table~\ref{tab:sphtotals}", but α=5° (Stock Fig. 14c,
   measured at 1.52×10⁶) and α=29.7° (Stock Fig. 16c, measured at 1.53×10⁶ — "for
   the small Reynolds number, transition is provoked completely by separation") are
   measured, fully solved on L0–L2, and sitting in the table two paragraphs up.
   Either add these two front comparisons (both are the same laminar-to-separation
   regime as the α=10° figure, so the digitization + `compare_stock_fronts.py`
   machinery applies directly) or name them in the deferral. As written, the
   scoping implies the only missing comparisons are unconverged runs, which is not
   the case.
