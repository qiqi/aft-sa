## New major comments

1. **The cavL1 α=15° non-convergence disclosure was deleted while the
   same commit puts that case into the headline figure.** The deleted
   parenthetical (old l. ~1521--1524) was the paper's only statement
   that `cavL1prop_nlf0416_Re4M_a15` hit the iteration budget with its
   lower front still wandering over 0.65--0.67c; the committed campaign
   JSON still marks it `converged: false` --- the sole non-converged
   case of the twenty-four --- and fig:nlfaft now plots its lower front
   (0.6704c, mid-size orange triangle) indistinguishably from the
   converged points. Cheapest repair: one clause in the fig:nlfaft
   caption ("the cavity-L1 15° lower front reached the iteration budget
   still wandering over 0.65--0.67c; the final batch's value is
   plotted"), or restore the parenthetical to the adversarial-mesh
   paragraph. As committed, the section's "twenty-four cases" and the
   Conclusion's "handover held ... on twenty-four cases" silently
   include a case the campaign artifact itself flags.

2. **fig:nlfpolar's tex caption was not updated for the two AFT curves
   the commit added to the plot.** The caption (l. 1528--1542)
   explicitly enumerates every curve family --- experiment, mfoil,
   FlexFoil diamonds, "the gray dash-dot curve is fully turbulent SA"
   --- but says nothing about the two new gray AFT (OVERFLOW) polars
   (dashed N_crit=10.07, solid 7.18), which sit adjacent to the other
   gray curve it *does* describe. Same staleness class as pass 19's
   major 2 (caption vs committed panel). While fixing it, two clauses
   are owed: the provenance (digitized from Coder's Fig. 5-25, same
   source/JSON as fig:nlfaft) and the truncation --- the digitized
   AFT polars are cut at the source figure's $c_d\!=\!0.025$ axis
   limit (the JSON's own notes say so), so the gray curves end
   mid-plot on an axis that runs to 0.04.

3. **"FlexFoil and XFOIL both converge at the identical conditions
   ($C_l\!=\!1.51$ at $\alpha\!=\!9^\circ$...)" (l. 1553--1556) makes an
   XFOIL claim no committed artifact supports --- and partially
   re-reverses the pass-17 attribution fix.** The only committed
   high-α NLF $e^9$ data, `xfoil_nlf0416_Re4M.pkl`, carries
   `source='flexfoil'` at all four incidences (the 1.5138 quoted is
   FlexFoil's), and the packaged xfoil still dies on the NLF input:
   rerun today with the walk-up ladder and graphics off, SIGFPE
   (rc −8); `xvfb-run`, which the README says the XFOIL scripts
   need, is not on this machine. The pass-17 round moved exactly this
   sentence from XFOIL to FlexFoil for exactly this reason. If the
   evidence is the ∂x_tr/∂N sweep (whose caption says XFOIL converged
   at 9°/15° "at the original study's conditions"), say that and drop
   the implication that XFOIL reproduces the 1.51: e.g. "FlexFoil
   converges at the identical conditions ($C_l\!=\!1.51$ ...), as does
   XFOIL in the $N_\mathrm{crit}$ sweep of
   Table~\ref{tab:nlftrans}." Otherwise commit an XFOIL-at-9° artifact.
