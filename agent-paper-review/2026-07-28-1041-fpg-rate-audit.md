**Rate verdict, quantified.** The model tracks Drela through the mild
favorable branch (0.74-1.00x at beta <= 0.10 — the published fig:nuhat
claim, reproduced), is HALVED at beta = +0.20, and **collapses by three
orders of magnitude between beta = 0.2 and 0.5** (0.001x at beta = 0.5;
identically zero growth at plane stagnation — the eigenvalue is negative
at every Re_theta up to 16000, i.e. the disturbance DECAYS on profiles
where Drela's envelope floor still demands >= 5.7e-3 per Re_theta once
supercritical). The cliff is the kernel coordinate itself: on
non-inflectional accelerated profiles I_hat = (Y-X-Z)/R goes to zero
(curvature Z > 0 eats the margin), so a = a_max*clip(P) starves
regardless of a_max. The user's "abysmally low" is confirmed and located:
H <= ~2.35 (beta >= ~0.3), where the correlations' floor keeps amplifying
but the kernel does not.

**Onset (secondary, per the correction).** Through beta = +0.20 the gate
is NOT the binding constraint — it opens BEFORE Drela's critical
Re_theta0 everywhere on the mild branch (166 vs 242 at Blasius; 542 vs
827 at +0.10; 1010 vs 1642 at +0.20), and the transported N=1 crossing
lands within +-11% of Drela's implied N=1 station. At beta >= +0.5 the
P-dependent threshold is formally late (band value 24680 vs Drela 3757 at
+0.5) BUT the saturated ceiling Re_Omega = 1851.2 is presented by
Re_theta ~ 1150-1280 (well before Drela's critical) — the gate would open
in a thick-enough layer; there is simply no rate behind it. Rate-starved,
not gate-blocked, exactly as the correction framed it.

**Consequence for the tuning plan: the high-Re cylinder is the WRONG
instrument for the FPG rate.** No e^N-faithful FPG retune (i.e. one that
respects Drela as its target, as this model's calibration does) can move
the cylinder front forward of ~79 deg at 2e7 — the correlations
themselves forbid it. A model that DID transition on the nose would be
adding bypass content, not fixing FPG amplification. The cases that
would actually constrain the FPG rate are supercritical attached FPG
layers: Re_theta > Re_theta0(H) with H = 2.30-2.45, e.g. the beta =
0.2-0.5 wedges at Re_theta ~ 2000-8000 as numerical targets (where the
deficiency is a clean factor 2 -> 1000), or any high-Re NLF-type body
whose favorable run reaches Re_theta ~ 4000 before the pressure minimum.

## Tuning implications (SKETCHED ONLY — nothing implemented, per brief)

1. **Not a_max**: rate = a_max*clip(P)*S; with P ~ 1e-3 no ceiling
   rescale reaches 5e-3-class envelope slopes without breaking the
   Michalke anchor (a_max is an eigenvalue, Sec. II.C).
2. **Not the gate constants (124.6 / 1.424 / 1851.2)**: the gate is
   already open (or opens pre-Drela-critical) everywhere the rate fails;
   retuning them moves onset, not growth. They also cannot produce the
   missing cylinder-nose fronts (rate ~ 0 behind an open gate).
3. **The kernel's I_hat content on non-inflectional FPG layers is the
   deficient element.** Candidate functional forms the beta = 0.2-0.5
   wedge data would constrain: (i) a P-floor tied to the open gate —
   a >= a_floor * S(Re_Omega/Re_Omega_c) with a_floor calibrated to
   Drela's 5e-3 envelope floor via the frozen eigenvalue (the
   amplifying-zone / K-gate revision class, 1250/1315 memos — follow-on
   model work, explicitly out of scope for this paper); (ii) softening
   the -Z (curvature) penalty in I_hat = (Y-X-Z)/R for Z > 0 (favorable
   curvature), which is exactly the term that kills P between beta = 0.2
   and 0.5 while H only moves 2.41 -> 2.30. Either would be calibrated on
   the ladder table above, NOT on the cylinder (which cannot see the
   difference below 79 deg).
4. If transcritical-cylinder realism is itself the goal, the missing
   ingredient is a bypass-class mechanism (roughness/Tu receptivity),
   plus the 0412 turbulent-separation deficiency for Cd — both outside
   the FPG rate question.

## Caveats (recorded)

1. The march runs on the RANS Cp, which already contains the model's own
   front feedback (post-front Cp shape differs from a truly-laminar
   solution aft of ~80-90 deg); nose-region conclusions (theta < 78 deg)
   are insensitive — Cp there is smooth and near-potential (u_e 3-5%
   below 2 sin theta, consistent with steady-wake displacement).
2. H(theta) carries ~+-0.02 jitter on the highre-mesh cases (surface-Cp
   wiggle through savgol); the critical-Re_theta margin on the nose is
   x2.5-3, far above the jitter.
3. 2e7 case runs at y+max = 1.4 (0412 stretch caveat) — its Cp/front
   enter this audit as-is.
4. maxP values below ~1e-3 are at the estimator noise floor (savgol vs
   exact-gradient disagree there; both read "zero" physically).
5. Solver fronts quoted are the campaign's near-wall chi=1 convention
   (matrix_summary.jsonl); the 1033 radial-ray convention differs by
   ~1 deg on the high arm (83.0 vs 82 at 2e7) — no verdict changes.
6. beta = 1 marched row: the domain-sizing loop tops out with N_end ~
   0 e-folds at Re_theta ~ 1e6-class domains — reported as "never"
   rather than a number; the frozen eigenvalue (negative at all Rt)
   carries the quantitative statement.
