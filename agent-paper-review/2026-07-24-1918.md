1. **"The predecessor-kernel campaign sat 11–17 counts below the same
   reference" (l. 2611–2613) is true only at α=4°.** Against the
   corrected reference the predecessor L1/L2 deficits are: α=4°
   12.1–16.7 counts below; α=5° 4.2–10.7 below; α=6° 2.5 below to
   **5.0 above** (cavity L1: 0.02464 vs 0.02414). The 11–17 range is
   what you get by subtracting the α=4° reference correction (29.0
   counts) from the old campaign-wide 40–46-count deficit — but the
   correction grows with α (35.3/42.3 counts), which is exactly why the
   per-α recomputation collapses the α=6° deficit to zero. As written
   the parenthetical also fights the adjacent "returned 6–14 counts"
   claim (11−14…17−6 would put the final kernel at −3…+11 *below*,
   while it actually lands −7 to +11 straddling the reference). Fix:
   scope it ("up to 17 counts below at α=4°, the deficit shrinking with
   incidence to ≈0 at 6°") or quote the full range (−5 to +17-count
   spread vs the final kernel's ±11/±3).

2. **The signed-algebra paragraph relocated to Sec. III (l. 1253–1283)
   has lost its antecedent.** It opens "How these magnitude-based
   components realize the signed algebra of Sec. II is worth stating
   precisely" and immediately uses "the implemented triple" and
   "$Y=\omega d$" — but at this point in the paper the reader has only
   seen the *signed* triple $(u, du', \tfrac12 d^2u'')$ of
   Eq. (indicators); the magnitude-based implementation
   $(|\mathbf u|, \omega d, \tfrac12 d^2\hat{\mathbf n}\!\cdot\!\nabla\omega)$
   is defined only in Appendix E (where this paragraph used to live,
   directly after that definition). The paragraph even precedes the
   flat-plate implementation note that first mentions
   $(\nabla^2\mathbf u)\cdot\hat{\mathbf u}$. Fix: one setup sentence
   ("The solver evaluates the indicators from magnitudes —
   $|\mathbf u|$, $\omega d$, and the projected curvature — rather than
   signed profile derivatives (assembled in Appendix E); how these ...")
   and/or move the paragraph after the implementation note.

   counts", "±3 counts" without "within", or "(1.5%)"→"(≈1.5%)" would

   calculator.
2. **Two small physics wordings in the new II.E inner-layer paragraph
   (l. 1133–1138)**: (a) "$Re_\Omega\!=\!y^+/\kappa$" is the log-layer
   asymptote only — in the sublayer $Re_\Omega = d^2\omega/\nu = y^{+2}$
   (larger than $y^+/\kappa$ below $y^+\!\approx\!6$); the conclusion
   survives easily (band max ≈120 vs the k·C ceiling 1851) but the
   formula as stated is not "the local $Re_\Omega$" across the whole
   band. (b) "$P_\mathrm{AI}$ is identically zero" is exact where
   $\hat S g\le 0$ (sublayer and log layer — the clip returns 0), but in
   the buffer $\hat S g$ can be marginally positive and $P_\mathrm{AI}$
   is merely negligible (onset gate ≈10⁻³, and the $\max$ selects the
   SA branch regardless). "Negligible — the clip and the onset gate
