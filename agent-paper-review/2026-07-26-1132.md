1. **The spheroid text claims a computed L0–L2 ladder, but L2 has not landed.**
   "on a structured O-grid ladder (L0--L2: 0.37/2.2/12.6 million nodes, ...)"
   (l. ~2719) reads as three computed solutions; only L0 and L1 are complete —
   `spheroid_fv1/case_ogrid_L2_saai_a10` is mid-run (pseudo-step ~9,100/20,000,
   CL still transient at 0.158 vs L1's 0.208). Either scope the sentence to the
   computed levels ("L0/L1 computed, the 12.6 M-node L2 in progress") or hold the
   section until L2 lands. Related: L0→L1 moves CL +1.4 % and CD +4.3 % — not yet
   grid-converged; when L2 completes, the figure should probably show the finest
   level (or the ladder), and any convergence statement must quote the three-level
   numbers.

2. **"to follow" is not submission-safe.** "the maps here are the infrastructure
   for that comparison, with the measured-front overlays and the workshop
   condition ($Re_L=6.5\times10^6$) to follow" (l. ~2727) is fine as a draft
   marker but promises the paper's own forthcoming content. Before submission it
   must either be fulfilled (overlays in) or re-cast as outlook/future work.
   As it stands the section validates nothing — it demonstrates presentation only.
   Consistent with the commit's "DRAFT" labeling; tracking it here so it cannot
   ship silently.

3. **Standing-instruction deferral, recorded now so it isn't lost**: when the
   measured-front overlays go in, the Kreplin/Krimmelbein source figures must be
   independently digitized (Krimmelbein 2021, elib.dlr.de, is the identified open
   digitization source) and cross-checked against whatever overlay the regen
   script produces — and the exact experimental Reynolds number of the overlaid
   map must be pinned at that point (see minor 7).
