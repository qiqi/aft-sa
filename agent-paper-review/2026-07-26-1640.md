## New major comments

1. **"At every azimuth" is contradicted by the commit's own diagnostic
   data.** The new sentence claims "the χ=1 and χ=c_v1 crossings nearly
   coincide here (Δx ≤ 0.02 L, both at 0.90–0.97 x/L at every azimuth)".
   On the full L2 φ-grid the gap reaches 0.049 L (φ=75°) and exceeds 0.02 L
   at 11 of 121 azimuths, and the crossings span 0.896–0.989 x/L (past
   0.97 for φ≳136°, below 0.90 at φ=66–76.5°). Both quantifiers hold only
   at the 8 measured azimuths the table prints. The conclusion is
   unaffected (0.049 L max handover width ≪ the 0.13–0.56 L amplification
   gap), so the fix is wording: "at the measured azimuths" or the honest
   full-grid numbers (≤0.05 L, 0.90–0.99). The same-sentence tension with
   the retained pass-40 "stays aft (0.92–0.97 x/L) at every azimuth"
   (actual c_v1 full-grid range 0.915–0.989) resolves with the same edit.
2. **The trailing causal clause is unprobed (pass-31 class, clause-scoped).**
   "The wall-anchored rate kernel books only weak growth in the lifted
   layer" asserts a specific kernel-level cause, but no committed
   diagnostic probes the rate (Ω̂Î, or the Re_Ω vs Re_Ω^c gate) along the
   flank, and two in-model alternatives are live: (a) the in-bubble arm of
   Eq. (reomc) — which the paper itself credits (Daedalus passage,
   l. ~2706) with *deliberately* suppressing the rate while a separated
   layer is wall-damped — and (b) plain sub-critical Re at 1.5×10⁶
   (Stock's own reading: laminar to separation at this Re, fronts tracking
   the separation line — under which the "deficit" is partly physics, not
   defect). Additionally, `surface_map.py` samples χ only on rays to
   0.04 L off the wall (`CHI_RAY` max), so "weak growth in the lifted
   layer" is verified only within 0.04 L; growth in a layer lifted beyond
   that would be invisible to chimax. Recommend: keep the
   diagnostic-backed statement (χ=1 itself arrives 0.13–0.56 L late, so
   the deficit is in booked amplification, not handover) and either
   soften the kernel clause to a hypothesis or commit a flank rate/gate
   probe.
