## New major comments

1. **The caption's headline claim — "the untuned SA-AI fronts sit
   inside it … at every incidence" — is unsupported at α=9°, one of
   the four incidences the figure plots.** The digitized envelope's α
   domain is −4…8° (13 points; the source panels' axes end at 8°),
   so the upper-surface 9° cluster (x_t/c = 0.010–0.105) sits to the
   right of the shaded band with nothing around it: no envelope, no
   experiment circle (last recoverable point α=7.13°), and no
   SA-LM2015 (Piotrowski's sweep ends at 6°). "On the
   experiment–Langtry–Menter cluster … at every incidence" fails
   there too, for the same reason. The band's 8° floor (0.008) does
   suggest the 9° points *would* be inside an extended band, but the
   figure cannot show that. Fix is one clause, not data: either drop
   the 9° points from this figure (they already live in fig:nlfaft),
   or scope the sentence — "inside it at every incidence the envelope
   covers (−4°…8°)" — and let the −4° sentence carry the edge case
   as it already does.

## New minor comments

1. **Workshop name inconsistency**: the caption says "1st AIAA
   Transition Modeling and Prediction Workshop"; the workshop's name
   (and the paper's own bib entry, `coder_tmpw_summary`) includes
   "CFD" — "First AIAA CFD Transition Modeling and Prediction
   Workshop". Add "CFD" in the caption.
2. **"The envelope's width---0.4–0.6 in x_t/c at fixed
   incidence---is the workshop's headline result" overstates on both
   ends.** Actual per-incidence widths from the committed JSON span
   0.27 (upper, 8°) to 0.66 (upper, −4°); "0.4–0.6" describes the
   mid-α band only. And "the workshop's headline result" is an
   attribution the cited deck can't be quoted for (the Case-2B pages
   are image-only slides; the extractable text gives only the
   submittal count). Suggest "reaches 0.4–0.6 in x_t/c" and either
   soften "headline" or attach it to a quotable sentence in Coder's
   written summary if one exists.
3. **Piotrowski provenance is one document removed from the
   citation.** The digitization source is the saved 2020
   manuscript/preprint (`references/PiotrowskiZingg2020_…pdf`, whose
   p. 22 caption "Fig. 6 NLF0416 grid convergence … and angle of
   attack sweep" I confirmed), but the caption cites the 2021 AIAA
   Journal version and says "their Fig.~6" — the journal layout's
   figure numbering was not verified. Also, Fig. 6(f)'s native axes
   are (x/c, C_l); the plotted α values are the assumed −6…6°-step-2
   sweep (recorded and sanity-checked in the JSON via
   c_l(0°)=0.497), which the caption doesn't mention. One clause
   ("replotted against the sweep's α; their Fig. 6(f) uses c_l")
   plus a figure-number check against the journal PDF closes both.
4. **"The full recomputation is in progress" (II.F status sentence)
   is already stale**: per RESPONSES.md (~19:20 UTC, same day) the
   canon `flow360_fv1` campaign was deliberately PAUSED at ~20/93 to
   put the GPUs on the invariant-kernel debug. Either reword to
   something pause-proof ("is underway and will be adopted
   wholesale" has the same problem; "will be recomputed and adopted
   wholesale" does not) or accept the maintenance burden.
5. **The battery record backing the II.F bounds is not committed as
   data.** The 1.5-count and "toward the measurement" claims trace
   only to untracked run directories and `runlogs/fv1b_battery.log`
   — and that log is incomplete (no DONE lines for the epp60k/100k/
   300k slots; their results live only in run-dir CSVs). By the
   paper's own digitization standard (committed JSON per quoted
   dataset), a small committed `fv1bypass_battery_results.json`
   (17 slots × on/off × CL/CD) would make the sentence verifiable
   from the repo.
6. **Cosmetics in the rendered figure**: the legend prints a literal
   double hyphen ("Piotrowski--Zingg" — matplotlib doesn't apply TeX
   ligatures), and the SA-AI O-grid squares share color C0 with the
   envelope band fill/edges (blue-on-blue; distinguishable but the
   one marker family that can vanish into the band edge at −4°).
7. **Lower-surface 9° is silently omitted** (caption states the
   selection but not the reason). The campaign has the points (cav
   0.62–0.64, str 0.63–0.64, plus a strL0 outlier at 0.29 of the
   under-resolved-front class fig:nlfaft already disclaims). Since
